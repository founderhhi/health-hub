# Design Decisions — Confirmed by Stakeholder

## Theme Consistency
- **Both light and dark mode must exist for everything.** If a variation proposes a change, it must work in both themes. No dark-only or light-only treatments.
- The dark AI summary section in the GP dashboard is intentional differentiation — keep it, but ensure it also has a proper light-mode counterpart.

## AI Section
- The AI section is a **high-importance, showcase feature**. Give it visual prominence and polish across all roles where it appears.
- Treat AI surfaces as premium moments — they should feel distinctive and memorable.

## Sparklines & Data Visualization
- Sparklines represent **real data placeholders** — they will carry real patient/operational data at scale.
- Design with the assumption of rich data. Use the full real estate available for data visualization.
- These are not decorative — they are functional data displays that will grow.

## Queue Density
- **20-40 patients/day is the right density target** for GP queue design. Design for this volume.
- This is ambitious but realistic for the product's trajectory.

## Platform per Role
- **Patient portal: Mobile-first** — design variations should prioritize mobile, with desktop as secondary.
- **All other roles (GP, Specialist, Pharmacy, Diagnostics, Admin): Desktop/web-first** — design variations should prioritize desktop density and efficiency. Mobile is secondary for provider roles.
