# Scenario 1 - Level 1 (Ad Hoc / Bootstrapped)

## Scenario Summary
Self-funded only. The scenario keeps the previously approved checkpoint shape, but the underlying staffing, rate-card, and operating model now reflect a part-time multi-team organization with explicit doctor, admin, and support coverage.

- Economic cost to Production: $451,751 / INR 37,495,333
- Target timeline to Production: 34 months
- Founder/company capital modeled before outside funding: $240,000 / INR 19,920,000
- Investor capital modeled: None
- Main risk to watch: timeline drag and callback fatigue before scale economics kick in

## Risk Register and Contingencies

| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Funding delay | Low | High | Slow hiring, stretch manual ops, and prioritize doctor/admin coverage before marketing expansion. |

## Assumption Notes
- Android is still modeled from the external quote: 480 total hours at $15/hr average across the mobile team.
- Remaining web work is normalized to roughly 150%-160% of Android effort, with an expected case of 745 hours, while the rest of the technical time supports hardening, QA, training, and launch operations.
- Salary benchmarks used for employed coverage: NHM Assam Medical Officer (MBBS) remuneration band (~INR 54,625-57,881 per month, official RoP approval), NCS customer/back-office roles (~INR 13,000-22,000), and NCS technical-support roles (~INR 18,000-28,000). Planning model uses INR 60,000 for doctors, INR 18,000 for admin ops, and INR 25,000 for technical support.
