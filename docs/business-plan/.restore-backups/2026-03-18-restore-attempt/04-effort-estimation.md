# 04 — Effort Estimation

**Health Hub Business Plan | Updated Effort Model**

## Executive Summary

The remaining effort is now modeled around a smaller and more realistic web-hardening estimate, backed by the revised staffing plan:

- Remaining web product work: **720-770 hours**
- Android APK: **480 hours** (fixed external quote)
- Additional hardening / operations setup work: **660 hours**
- Combined remaining effort basis: **~1885 hours**

| Workstream | Hours | Notes |

## Team Capacity Assumptions

| Level | Productive Technical Capacity | Interpretation |
| --- | --- | --- |

| Level 2 | ~320 hrs/mo | Balanced utilization; default planning case |
| Level 3 | ~480 hrs/mo | Full effective use of the 7-developer part-time pool |

## Why the Timelines Are Not Cut in Half

The user-supplied team estimate reduces pure web development effort, but the plan still preserves meaningful runway for:

- doctor training and callback process design
- admin operations setup and refreshers
- QA, release governance, and production hardening
- partner onboarding, reporting, and manual-fallback readiness

That is why the revised model lowers technical effort without pretending the whole business becomes launch-ready the moment the code compiles.
