# 01 — Timeline

**Health Hub Business Plan | Updated Timeline Document**

This timeline keeps the broad checkpoint structure previously approved, but now explicitly runs non-code operating work in parallel with product delivery: doctor training, admin training, callback protocols, escalation SOPs, support readiness, and launch playbooks.

## Shared Parallel Workstreams

| Parallel Track | When It Runs | Why It Matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Doctor training protocols | Pre-Pilot onward | Needed so live pilot quality does not depend only on code completion |
| Admin callback and fulfilment SOPs | Pre-Pilot onward | Critical because travel, diagnostics, pharmacy, and exceptions remain partly manual |
| Technical support runbooks | Pilot 1 onward | Required once real users and partner facilities are live |
| Release governance and checklists | Pre-Pilot through Production | Prevents part-time team fragmentation during launch |

## Timeline Logic

- The major phase order is unchanged: Pre-Pilot → Pilot 1 → Pilot 2 → Production Readiness.
- The reduced web-effort assumption (720-770 hours) does **not** fully collapse the timelines, because the saved technical effort is partly reallocated into hardening, QA, training, and operational setup.
- Android remains a fixed parallel critical path at 480 hours total.

## Timeline Interpretation

1. Scenario 3 remains the cleanest way to reach Production within roughly 16-20 months because investor support begins right after Pilot 1.
2. Scenario 2 remains the operational fallback because it preserves the same structure but pushes heavier staffing and polishing later.
3. Scenario 1 is still possible, but the longer duration now visibly compounds founder-office and care-ops cost even when monthly spend feels lean.

## Recommended Timeline View

- **Primary path:** S3-O2-I2 (20 months)
- **Fallback path:** S2-O2-I2 (24 months)
- **Upside path:** S3-O2-I3 (18 months)
