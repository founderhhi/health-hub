# Health Hub Master Business Plan

## Executive Summary
Health Hub already has a real multi-role prototype in code. This updated plan preserves the approved launch logic, but replaces the older staffing and costing assumptions with a more realistic structure: 3 part-time core team members, 7 part-time developers, 2 fractional advisors, fixed Android costing, smaller remaining web effort, and explicit employed clinical/admin/support coverage during pilots.

## Revised Planning Assumptions
- No tenant-isolated redesign in this phase.
- Patient APK stays the only mobile build; provider users remain on web.
- Major phase windows stay intact, but training, callback SOPs, and operational guidelines now run explicitly in parallel.
- The economic model counts all labor at market value even when friendly networks reduce near-term cash payment.
- Equity is modeled as 60% owner/founder, 20% founding+strategic pool, 20% investor pool.

## Updated Baseline
The balanced primary path is **S3-O2-I2** at **$276,996 / INR 22,990,668** through Production, with **$95,000 / INR 7,885,000** before outside funding and **$120,000 / INR 9,960,000** from a core pre-seed style round.

## Recommendation Framework
1. **Primary path:** S3-O2-I2 because it balances timing, staffing realism, and dilution from the dedicated investor pool.
2. **Fallback path:** S2-O2-I2 because it preserves the same operating model if investor timing slips beyond Pilot 1.
3. **Upside path:** S3-O2-I3 if the investor is strategic and the business can absorb faster fixed-cost expansion without losing discipline.

## Key Risk Shifts in This Revision
- Manual ops are no longer undercounted; employed admin and clinical coverage are now explicit.
- Founder office time is materially larger than before because all three core members are costed at market value.
- APK cost risk is lower than before because the Android quote is now fixed instead of back-solving from inflated blended rates.
