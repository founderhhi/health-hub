# 03 — Cost Breakdown

**Health Hub Business Plan | Updated Cost Model**

## What Changed

This revision updates the cost stack to reflect:

- the new rate card ($10 junior, $20 senior, $15 default, $20 management)
- the larger but part-time team model
- explicit employed coverage for doctors, admin ops, and technical support
- higher infrastructure / phone / callback tooling assumptions
- a smaller web-effort assumption and fixed Android quote

## Monthly Phase Cost Logic

| Phase | Fixed Founder Office | Adjustable Product / Infra / Tooling | Explicit Employed Ops |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pre-Pilot | $5,200/mo | Web, QA, tooling, legal, partner prep | Training only |
| Pilot 1 | $5,200/mo | Web, QA, partner prep, launch tooling | 2 doctors + 2 admin + 2 support |
| Pilot 2 | $5,200/mo | Web, QA, reporting, infra growth | 4 doctors + 4 admin + 2 support |
| Production Readiness | $5,200/mo | Final hardening, monitoring, launch tooling | 6 doctors + 6 admin + 3 support |

## Cost Interpretation

1. Lower monthly cash intensity does not automatically mean lower total economic cost; long timelines compound founder-office and care-ops spend.
2. Android is materially cheaper in this revision because it is now locked to the quoted 480-hour, $15/hr model.
3. Explicit doctor/admin/support staffing raises realism in the pilot phases and removes an under-modeled operating burden from the earlier version.
