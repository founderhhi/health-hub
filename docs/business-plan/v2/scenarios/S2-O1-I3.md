# Scenario S2-O1-I3 — Our Level 1 (Bootstrapped) / Investor Level 3 (Fully Funded)

## Scenario Summary

Maximum-leverage path. The founders self-fund at bootstrapped intensity (~40% of ideal spend) through Pre-Pilot and Pilot 1, then a fully funded investor arrives during the Pilot 1 to Pilot 2 transition (approximately M9-M10). The investor's capital supercharges operational capacity to corporate-level resourcing, enabling aggressive parallel execution in Pilot 2 and rapid Production hardening. Founder exposure is minimized while execution speed approaches the fastest S2 timelines. The trade-off is higher dilution and investor governance expectations.

- **Economic cost to Production:** $190,000-240,000 / INR 15,770,000-19,920,000
- **Target timeline to Production:** 22-26 months
- **Founder/company capital before investor:** $36,000-50,000 / INR 2,988,000-4,150,000
- **Investor capital modeled:** $154,000-190,000 / INR 12,782,000-15,770,000
- **Equity structure:** 60 / 20 / 20 (founder / founding team / investor pool)
- **Main risk to watch:** bootstrapped founder phase may signal low skin-in-the-game to a corporate-level investor; high post-investor burn rate demands revenue traction by M18-M20

## Recommendation

S2-O1-I3 is a **medium-high risk, moderate-speed combination** within Scenario 2. It minimizes founder capital exposure (~$58,000) while leveraging a corporate-level investor (~$172,000) to compress Pilot 2 and Production Readiness into 15 months of aggressive execution. The 22-26 month total timeline is 4-8 months faster than S2-O1-I1 and 2-4 months faster than S2-O1-I2.

**This path is viable but requires a specific investor profile.** It should be pursued if:
1. An investor is available who is willing to deploy $154,000-190,000 into a pre-revenue East Africa health-tech company based on Pilot 1 data
2. The investor accepts a term structure within the 20% equity pool (or the founder is willing to negotiate pool expansion)
3. The founding team can articulate a compelling narrative that compensates for the low personal capital investment (sweat equity + prototype value + operational traction)
4. The team has the coordination capacity to absorb a jump from bootstrapped to corporate-level resourcing without execution dysfunction

**Key advantage over S2-O1-I2:** Corporate-level resourcing compresses Pilot 2 from 8 months to 6 months and Production from 10 months to 9 months. Near-production staffing from Pilot 2 onward reduces operational risk and enables dual-market preparation.

**Key disadvantage vs. S2-O1-I2:** Higher dilution (20% vs. 16.7%), higher total economic cost ($215,000 vs. $157,500), full consumption of the investor pool with no reserve for follow-on, and higher governance expectations from a corporate-level investor.

**Key disadvantage vs. S2-O2-I2 or S2-O2-I3:** Bootstrapped Pilot 1 quality may not justify the corporate-level ask. If the investor needs polished metrics and operational maturity to commit $172,000, the bootstrapped early phase may be the weakest link in the pitch.
