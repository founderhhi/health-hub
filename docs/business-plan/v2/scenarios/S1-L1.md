# Scenario 1 - Level 1 (Bootstrapped / Ad Hoc) — Fully Self-Funded

## Scenario Summary

Maximum-constraint path. All capital comes from founders. Team operates at ~40% of ideal utilization (~320 productive technical hrs/mo). Every phase stretches because work is heavily sequenced rather than parallelized, and operational workstreams compete with the same founder hours. No investor dilution, but runway risk is acute throughout.

- Economic cost to Production: **$432,500 / INR 35,897,500**
- Target timeline to Production: **34 months**
- Founder capital required: **$232,000 / INR 19,256,000**
- Investor capital: **$0**
- Equity: 60 / 20 / 0 (20% investor pool reserved, unissued)
- Main risk to watch: founder burnout and cash exhaustion before revenue covers burn

## Recommendation

**Not recommended as a standalone path.** S1-L1 is the survival-mode scenario. The 34-month timeline, negative cash position by M15, and accumulated founder burden make this the highest-risk combination in the matrix.

This path is viable only if:
1. Founders have personal savings exceeding $250,000 and are willing to absorb negative cash flow for 12+ months
2. The team treats this as a side project with no urgency to capture market timing
3. There is a credible fallback plan to convert to S2 or S3 if the M15 cash trigger is hit

If none of these conditions hold, the founder should pursue S1-L2 at minimum or seek early-stage investment (S2 or S3). This scenario exists primarily as a lower bound for planning — it shows what happens if no outside capital materializes and the team operates at minimum capacity.
