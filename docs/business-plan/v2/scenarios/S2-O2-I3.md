# Scenario S2-O2-I3 — Our Level 2 (Ideal) / Investor Level 3 (Fully Funded)

## Scenario Summary

Ideal founding effort meets a fully funded investor. The founders self-fund at ideal intensity (~$17,707/mo burn) through Pre-Pilot and Pilot 1, then a corporate-level investor arrives during the Pilot 1 to Pilot 2 transition (approximately M7-M8) and deploys 150-200%+ of ideal capital. This creates a scenario where strong early execution is followed by an accelerated scaling phase — the investor's capital enables full corporate-level resourcing for Pilot 2 and Production Readiness, compressing the timeline to 18-22 months. The risk profile drops to Medium because the abundance of scaling capital eliminates most resource-constraint risks, though it introduces over-capitalization and governance concerns.

- **Economic cost to Production:** $210,000-275,000 / INR 17,430,000-22,825,000
- **Target timeline to Production:** 18-22 months
- **Founder/company capital before investor:** $71,000-95,000 / INR 5,893,000-7,885,000
- **Investor capital modeled:** $139,000-180,000 / INR 11,537,000-14,940,000
- **Equity structure:** 60 / 20 / 20 (founder / founding team / investor pool)
- **Break-even:** Month 22-26
- **Risk level:** Medium
- **Main risk to watch:** over-capitalization risk — spending more than necessary before product-market fit is fully validated; investor may demand board seat or governance controls proportional to their larger capital deployment

## Recommendation

S2-O2-I3 is a **strong acceleration scenario** that pairs ideal founder investment with corporate-level investor capital. The 18-22 month timeline is among the fastest in the S2 family, and the full corporate staffing enables dual-market readiness (Ethiopia + Kenya) by Production. However, the high burn rate ($29,637-32,357/mo post-investor) and near-exhaustion of the investor pool create structural constraints that require careful management.

**This path is best when:**
1. The investor is enthusiastic and wants to deploy significant capital for faster market capture
2. Competitive pressure or market timing demands accelerated Production readiness
3. Kenya opportunity is maturing in parallel and dual-market launch is strategically valuable
4. The founding team can sustain 150% utilization for 15 months without burnout

**This path should be avoided when:**
1. Product-market fit is not yet validated (premature scaling risk)
2. The investor demands governance controls disproportionate to the stage
3. Follow-on capital may be needed (investor pool is nearly exhausted)
4. The founding team is showing fatigue signs (150% utilization will worsen it)

**Compared to S2-O2-I2 (Fallback):**
- 2-4 months faster to Production (22 vs 24 months)
- Higher total cost ($242,500 vs $187,500 = $55,000 more)
- Better SLAs and staffing levels throughout
- But: higher dilution (17.8% vs 15.3%), higher burn rate, less follow-on headroom
- The additional $55,000 buys 2-4 months of acceleration and dual-market readiness — a reasonable trade if the capital is available and the market demands speed

**Compared to S3-O2-I3 (Upside):**
- Identical investor level (I3) but later investor entry (S2 vs S3)
- S2-O2-I3 requires more founder capital ($71,000-95,000 vs $53,000-65,000)
- S2-O2-I3 has a slightly longer timeline (18-22 vs 16-20 months)
- But: S2-O2-I3 gives the investor more traction data, potentially commanding better terms
