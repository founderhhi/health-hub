# Scenario S3-O1-I3 — Our Level 1 (Bootstrapped) / Investor Level 3 (Fully Funded)

## Scenario Summary

Minimum founder exposure with maximum investor resourcing. The founders self-fund at bootstrapped intensity (~40% of ideal spend) through Pre-Pilot and Pilot 1 only (~5-7 months), then a fully funded investor arrives right after Pilot 1 completes (approximately M7-M8). The investor provides corporate-level capital (150-200%+ of ideal), enabling aggressive scaling, parallel workstreams, and the fastest possible execution from Pilot 2 onward. This creates the widest capital asymmetry of any S3-O1 path — the investor contributes ~85-90% of total capital while the founder contributes ~10-15%.

- **Economic cost to Production:** $200,000-255,000 / INR 16,600,000-21,165,000
- **Target timeline to Production:** 18-22 months
- **Founder/company capital before investor:** $21,000-32,000 / INR 1,743,000-2,656,000
- **Investor capital modeled:** $179,000-223,000 / INR 14,857,000-18,509,000
- **Equity structure:** 60 / 20 / 20 (founder / founding team / investor pool)
- **Main risk to watch:** extreme capital asymmetry raises skin-in-the-game concerns; investor may demand governance controls, board seats, or equity beyond the 20% pool ceiling

## Recommendation

S3-O1-I3 is a **conditionally viable but narratively challenging path**. It offers the combination of minimum founder capital exposure ($21,000-32,000) with maximum investor resourcing, producing the fastest S3-O1 timeline (18-22 months) and enabling dual-market expansion. However, the extreme capital asymmetry (investor provides ~85-90% of total capital) creates real challenges.

**This path is appropriate if:**
1. The founders have limited personal capital (under $35,000) but have cultivated a strong relationship with a high-conviction investor
2. The investor is motivated by the market opportunity (East Africa health-tech) rather than founder capital match requirements
3. The founding team can demonstrate substantial sweat equity — time invested, prototype built, Pilot 1 executed — as a credible substitute for financial capital
4. The investor is comfortable with early-stage risk in exchange for meaningful equity (18-20% from the pool, potentially more via negotiation)
5. Competitive pressure or market timing demands faster execution than S3-O1-I2 provides

**Key trade-off vs. S3-O1-I2:** An additional $55,000-75,000 in investor capital buys 4 months of timeline acceleration and enables dual-market operations, but at the cost of higher dilution (18-20% vs. 16.7-18.0%) and increased investor governance expectations. The incremental value per month of acceleration is ~$14,000-19,000 — reasonable if the competitive landscape demands speed.

**Key trade-off vs. S3-O2-I3 (Upside recommendation):** Both paths have the same investor contribution level (I3), but S3-O2-I3 has the founder at ideal investment level ($53,000-65,000), providing stronger negotiating position and a more balanced capital structure. For founders who can afford O2, S3-O2-I3 is strictly preferable.

**The exceptional narrative this path requires:** "We built the entire prototype ourselves, ran a live pilot with real patients, proved the model works — and now we need a partner with capital and conviction to scale this across East Africa. Our investment is our time, our expertise, and the 18 months of full-time-equivalent work already in this platform."
