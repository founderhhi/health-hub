# Scenario 1 - Level 2 (Ideal / Basic) — Fully Self-Funded

## Scenario Summary

Moderate-constraint path. All capital comes from founders. Team operates at 100% of ideal utilization (~480 productive technical hrs/mo). Phases run at midpoint durations with enough concurrency for parallel build and operating prep. No investor dilution, but founders must deploy significant personal capital. Operational and market risks remain the primary threats.

- Economic cost to Production: **$175,000 / INR 14,525,000**
- Target timeline to Production: **24 months**
- Founder capital required: **$175,000 / INR 14,525,000**
- Investor capital: **$0**
- Equity: 60 / 20 / 0 (20% investor pool reserved, unissued)
- Main risk to watch: founder capital depletion before revenue ramp covers burn; no external safety net if costs overrun

## Key Risks

| # | Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|------|-----------|--------|------------|
| 1 | **Founder capital depletion before break-even** | High | Critical | Founders must have $175,000+ available. Cash goes negative at M9; requires pre-committed reserves or lump-sum injections. Consider converting to S2/S3 if capital reserves fall below 3 months of burn. |
| 2 | **No external validation or safety net** | High | High | Without an investor, there is no external check on product-market fit assumptions. Build in explicit go/no-go gates at each phase transition. |
| 3 | **Founder burnout over 24 months** | Medium-High | High | Shorter than L1's 34 months but still a long self-funded journey. Higher burn means more financial stress. Build in explicit rest cycles and consider the S2 pivot if fatigue sets in. |
| 4 | **Partner and clinic attrition during extended pilot** | Medium | High | 8-month Pilot 2 still risks losing early partners. Keep engagement through regular check-ins and incremental value delivery. |
| 5 | **Market window closure** | Medium | High | 24-month timeline is competitive but East African health-tech landscape is evolving. Monitor competitor moves and be ready to accelerate via scenario conversion. |

## Recommendation

**Consider if founders have capital.** S1-L2 is the viable self-funded path. The 24-month timeline is competitive, the team operates at ideal utilization, and the total cost is meaningful but not unreasonable for founders with sufficient personal reserves.

This path is viable if:
1. Founders have personal savings or committed capital exceeding $200,000 (buffer above the $175,000 modeled cost)
2. Founders are willing to absorb a negative cash position starting at M9 and deepening through M24
3. The team can maintain energy and focus over a 24-month self-funded journey without investor validation
4. There is a credible fallback plan to convert to S2 or S3 if the M9 cash trigger proves unsustainable

**Key advantage over S1-L1:** 10 months faster, higher team utilization enables parallel execution, and the higher burn rate is offset by a shorter accumulation period — resulting in less total cost than L1's stretched timeline might suggest.

**Key disadvantage vs. S2/S3 paths:** Founders bear 100% of financial risk with no external safety net. If the market does not respond as modeled, there is no investor capital buffer to absorb the shortfall.
