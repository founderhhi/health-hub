# Scenario S2-O1-I2 — Our Level 1 (Bootstrapped) / Investor Level 2 (Ideal)

## Scenario Summary

Lean-start, strong-finish path. The founders self-fund at bootstrapped intensity (~40% of ideal spend) through Pre-Pilot and Pilot 1, then an ideal-level investor arrives during the Pilot 1 to Pilot 2 transition (approximately M9-M10). The investor's capital lifts operational capacity to baseline adequacy, enabling parallel execution in Pilot 2 and a credible Production push. The early constraint phase keeps founder exposure low but creates a narrow cash window at the Pilot 1 close.

- **Economic cost to Production:** $140,000-175,000 / INR 11,620,000-14,525,000
- **Target timeline to Production:** 24-30 months
- **Founder/company capital before investor:** $36,000-50,000 / INR 2,988,000-4,150,000
- **Investor capital modeled:** $104,000-125,000 / INR 8,632,000-10,375,000
- **Equity structure:** 60 / 20 / 20 (founder / founding team / investor pool)
- **Main risk to watch:** bootstrapped Pre-Pilot and Pilot 1 may produce underwhelming traction data, weakening the investor pitch; thin runway at M9 demands investor close by M10

## Recommendation

S2-O1-I2 is a **high-risk, moderate-speed combination** within Scenario 2. It keeps founder capital exposure low (~$58,000) by bootstrapping through Pre-Pilot and Pilot 1, then relies on an ideal-level investor (~$114,500) to fund the scaling phases. The 24-30 month timeline is meaningfully shorter than S2-O1-I1 (30 months) because the investor's capital enables parallel execution in Pilot 2 and faster Production hardening.

**This path is viable but requires precise investor timing.** It should be pursued if:
1. Founders have limited personal capital (under $60,000 available) but a warm investor pipeline
2. The investor is willing to commit at ideal levels (~$104,000-125,000) based on Pilot 1 data
3. The founding team can sustain 9 months of bootstrapped self-funding without burnout
4. The team treats Pilot 1 as both a product pilot and an investor demo — every metric must be tracked and presentable

**Key advantage over S2-O1-I1:** The ideal-level investor provides enough capital to run Pilot 2 and Production at proper staffing and infrastructure levels, eliminating the "bootstrapped on both sides" problem that makes S2-O1-I1 fragile.

**Key disadvantage vs. S2-O1-I3 or S2-O2-I2:** The bootstrapped Pre-Pilot and Pilot 1 may not produce the quality of traction data that a better-funded early phase would. If the investor needs strong metrics to commit, consider S2-O2-I2 (higher founder capital, better Pilot 1 quality).
