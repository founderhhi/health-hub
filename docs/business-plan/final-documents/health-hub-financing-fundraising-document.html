How to read dilution now

  • Bridge cases are small uses of the investor pool, not full priced-seed stories.
  • Core pre-seed cases are the cleanest external fundraising cases.
  • Acceleration cases spend deeper into the investor pool and should be justified by real strategic value-add, not just speed.

Why the capital ask changed

  • The old model undercounted real operating staff during pilots.
  • The new model no longer inflates Android through a mismatched blended rate.
  • The founder office is larger than before because all three core members are valued economically, even if not paid full cash now.
  • Infrastructure now includes phones, callback telecom, devices, and support tooling.
