Updated Investor Narrative

Health Hub is now modeled as a real operating company, not just a product build.

This revision keeps the original market and product thesis, but replaces the older staffing assumptions with a more realistic build-and-operations model: three part-time core members, seven part-time developers, two advisors, a fixed Android quote, and explicit employed doctor/admin/support coverage through the pilots.
