## 1. Timeline Philosophy

Three principles govern the revised timeline:

1. **The phase order stays intact.** Pre-Pilot -> Pilot 1 -> Pilot 2 -> Production Readiness remains the right major structure.
2. **Lower coding effort does not erase operating work.** The reduced web-effort assumption is real, but the saved time is partly consumed by QA, hardening, SOP writing, training, partner activation, and release governance.
3. **The APK is a parallel critical path.** Android is not a “later nice-to-have” in the current plan; it is a tracked workstream that should ideally reach Pilot 1 and absolutely reach Pilot 2.

## 5. How to Interpret the Timing Differences

### 5.1 Why Level 1 Paths Stretch So Much

They do not stretch only because “code is slower.” They stretch because:

- founder office time is spread across more responsibilities,
- manual operations stay informal for longer,
- staffing formalization happens later,
- partner onboarding, reporting, and support become bottlenecks sooner.

### 5.2 Why Level 3 Paths Shorten So Much

They shorten because:

- more parallel work can happen safely,
- support and operating burden are formalized earlier,
- hardening and launch preparation do not need to wait behind every feature task.

### 5.3 Why the Timelines Are Not Cut in Half

The restored logic matters here. The founder provided a real estimate that reduced web build time substantially. That correction was correct and is preserved. But the company is still building:

- a patient APK,
- a hardened web platform,
- a callback-enabled operating layer,
- staffing and training systems,
- launch governance and production support discipline.

That is why the timeline compresses, but not unrealistically.
